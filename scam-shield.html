Already sent money or shared a code?
  1. Open your bank or e-wallet’s official app and report it right now. Fast reports have the best chance of freezing the transfer.
  2. Change your passwords and turn on passkeys or app-based login. Log out other devices.
  3. Screenshot the chat, number, account name and receipts before anything is deleted.
  4. Report to the PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group or the NBI Cybercrime Division, and to your bank or e-wallet.
  5. Ignore anyone offering to “recover” your money for a fee. That is usually a second scam.

This is a checklist-style helper, not a guarantee. A low score doesn’t mean something is safe. When unsure, contact the company using the number inside its official app or on your card.
